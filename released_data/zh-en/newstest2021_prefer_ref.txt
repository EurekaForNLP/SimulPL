Yesterday, the website of the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs released a notice soliciting public opinions on the "Administrative Measures for the Transfer of Rural Land Management Rights (Revised Draft for Soliciting Opinions)" (hereinafter referred to as the "Draft for Opinions"), adding supervision and risk prevention for the transfer of land management rights obtained by social capitals such as industrial and commercial enterprises.
For the transfer and exchange of land contract rights, new management measures will be issued separately.
Newly revised, the "Rural Land Contract Law" clarifies the scope of land transfer, implements the "three rights" concept, separately sets up "land contract rights" and "land management rights," and distinguishes clearly between transfer, exchange of land contract rights, and land management rights transfer (lease, subcontract, shares, or other ways).
The draft points out that, under the context of "three rights separation," farmers only invest the land management rights for a certain period.\r
To prevent the risk of farmers losing their land, methods like preferred shares, lease first then share, and buyback have emerged in practice.
Theoretically, even if bankruptcy liquidation occurs, farmers only risk losing operating rights for a certain period, so returning contracted land to original contractors is legally unnecessary and conflicts with the Bankruptcy Law.
In this Draft, to implement the concept of "three rights division", effectively respond to the key points of the Rural Land Contract Law amendments, considering significant differences in parties, procedures, and supervision in land management rights and land contract rights transfer, the revision defines the transfer scope, focuses on land management rights transfer, including leasing and shareholding, and changes the regulation name from "Management Method for the Transfer of Rural Land Contract Management Rights" to "Management Method for the Transfer of Rural Land Management Rights".
The Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs said to implement the newly revised Rural Land Contract Law, further consolidate and improve the basic rural management system, deepen rural land system reform, and implement the "three rights separation" system, it revised the Rural Land Contract Management Rights Transfer Method and formed a draft.
Considering that preferred shares have already had relatively mature theoretical discussions in corporate law, it is recommended to adopt them as an advocacy regulation to prevent the risks of investing in landless areas.
